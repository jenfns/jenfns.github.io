# Site refresh proposal

This branch is a review space for a larger website refresh. The goal is a conservative update, not a redesign.

## Main editorial direction

The current site is clean and professional. The main update should make the current research center of gravity visible: automorphic forms and arithmetic representation theory remain the core, while computation, formal methods, AI literacy, and mechanistic interpretability are now active research and leadership threads.

Suggested homepage framing:

> My research lies at the intersection of number theory, representation theory, and computation. I study automorphic forms, special values of L-functions, and the arithmetic of algebraic groups, with a particular interest in settings where abstract structure can be made explicit enough to compute, verify, or learn. My current work includes paramodular forms and representations of GSp(4), quaternionic modular forms on exceptional groups, formal and computational approaches to mathematics, and mathematically grounded methods for understanding learned representations in AI systems.
